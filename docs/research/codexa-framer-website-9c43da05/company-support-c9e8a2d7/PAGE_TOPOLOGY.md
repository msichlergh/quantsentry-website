# Support Page Topology

1. Shared fixed 72px Codexa navigation.
2. Support contact section: 1200px desktop frame with 160px top offset, two equal columns, and a 128px lower spacer.
3. Left column: heading, support copy, demo prompt, and Contact Sales link.
4. Right column: five required fields, optional privacy checkbox, submit button, and truthful demo note.
5. Shared Codexa CTA/footer.

The contact section is static. Form focus, checkbox, submit validation, and button/link hover states are native or CSS-driven. The shared footer supplies the viewport-triggered motion.
